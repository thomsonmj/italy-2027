Trip at a glance

A celebratory Italy journey with a strong rhythm

The original Classic Celebration backbone stays intact: Rome for history and energy, Florence for art and Tuscany, and Venice for atmosphere. The presentation is rebuilt around clarity, pacing, and maps that are actually useful.

The trip is designed to feel full, not frantic: one major anchor each morning, a quick local bite, a deliberate reset, then flexible late-afternoon and evening exploration.

Stays

Best bases for this exact route

The goal is to make the first and last hour of every day easier. Location, air conditioning, elevator access, and luggage logistics matter more than oversized apartments.

Rome

Best fit: Piazza Navona / Campo de’ Fiori / Pantheon.

  • Already aligns perfectly with your central Rome itinerary.
  • Keep evening walks short and atmospheric.
  • Prioritize quiet side street + A/C.

Florence

Best fit: Duomo / Santa Maria Novella for logistics, or Santo Spirito for ambiance.

  • Duomo base minimizes morning reservation friction.
  • Santo Spirito gives the best local evening feel.
  • Two bathrooms + washer are high-value mid-trip.

Venice

Best fit: Cannaregio.

  • Excellent for local evenings and cicchetti.
  • Closer to Santa Lucia means easier luggage days.
  • Count stepped bridges from vaporetto stop to front door.

Booking Watchlist

The reservations that matter

2027 inventory and exact release windows are not all published yet, so this keeps the priorities clear without pretending today’s rules are permanent.

  1. Florence lodging + Venice lodging: book refundable as soon as the right units appear.
  2. High-speed trains: Rome → Florence and Florence → Venice once June inventory opens.
  3. Florence icons: Duomo climb, Accademia, and Uffizi/Vasari; let official Vasari pairing dictate the Uffizi entry.
  4. Vatican Museums: target first-entry timing.
  5. Colosseum Full Experience: use the official 2027 release cadence when published.
  6. Venice: St. Mark’s, Doge’s Secret Itineraries, and Row Venice.
  7. Week-of-travel check: regional train times, Venice access-fee rules, opening hours, water taxi, and weather.
