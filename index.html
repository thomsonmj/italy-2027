Trip at a glance

A celebratory Italy journey with a strong rhythm

The Classic Celebration backbone stays intact: Rome for history and energy, Florence for art and Tuscany, and Venice for atmosphere. The maps now use Google for actual pedestrian routing while Apple Maps remains available as an iPhone-friendly handoff.

The trip is designed to feel full, not frantic: one major anchor each morning, a quick local bite, a deliberate reset, then flexible late-afternoon and evening exploration.

Stays

Best bases for this exact route

Location, air conditioning, elevator access, and luggage logistics matter more than oversized apartments.

Rome

Best fit: Piazza Navona / Campo de’ Fiori / Pantheon.

  • Excellent fit with the central Rome itinerary.
  • Short evening walks after full sightseeing days.
  • Prioritize quiet side street + A/C.

Florence

Best fit: Duomo / Santa Maria Novella for logistics, or Santo Spirito for ambiance.

  • Duomo minimizes morning reservation friction.
  • Santo Spirito gives the strongest local evening feel.
  • Two bathrooms + washer are high-value mid-trip.

Venice

Best fit: Cannaregio.

  • Closer to Santa Lucia means easier luggage days.
